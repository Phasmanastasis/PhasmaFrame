# Market Validation & Sizing

> Source issue: **PHASM-16**. Parent: PHASM-11. Milestone: MVP.

Validate the specific need for offline patient-to-BHW transfer and define a credible
reachable segment **before** estimating TAM/SAM/SOM.

## Current status

**No BHW/RHU interviews and no validated market-size estimate are documented.** The Quick
output is a research synthesis, not proof of demand. Do not invent interview findings or
repeat unsupported statistics. The suggested 10 interviews are **not** completed research.

## Validation questions (if time permits)

Ask BHWs / RHU staff:

- How are hypertension readings recorded today?
- When is the patient's history unavailable during a visit?
- How are readings transferred when internet is absent?
- Which phones / tablets are available?
- Who would approve, fund, maintain, and use this workflow?
- What current workaround already works?

## Sizing method (not yet executed)

1. Define the **reachable segment** as BHWs serving far-flung or low-connectivity
   households where compatible Android devices are available for **both** record entry and
   manual transfer.
2. Establish geography and device/user counts from **traceable sources** before any
   TAM/SAM/SOM calculation.
3. Note that the buyer/payer is **unvalidated**.

> TAM/SAM/SOM are intentionally left blank until sourced inputs exist. Filling them with
> guesses would violate the evidence boundary.
